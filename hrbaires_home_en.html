The problem

The hire failed before the search started.

It's rarely a candidate problem. The role was never actually clear before the search began — the title was inflated, the comp didn't match the market, or the structure wasn't built for next year.

And that mistake doesn't cost a salary. It costs a quarter — the team that takes the hit, the search you have to run again, and the seat still empty six months later.

Services

Two pillars, built for how you actually hire.

01

Recruiting

We define the role, the comp, and the org structure before we search. Skip that step and you're not solving your hiring problem — you're postponing it.

02

Staffing / Staff Augmentation

Embedded recruiting for companies hiring across LatAm — without building a local HR function from scratch.

Executive Search, Hiring Advisory, and RPO aren't extra line items — they're what Recruiting and Staffing naturally become when the need calls for it.

How we work

The process behind every placement.

01

We push back on the brief.

"You said CTO — you actually need a Head of Technology." That conversation happens before the search, not after the hire fails.

02

Calibration, not a close attempt.

Your first 3 candidates adjust the search in real time based on your reaction.

03

We stay until the offer's signed.

Not just until you say yes to a resume — through your full internal process.

04

Active follow-up.

Week 1 and month 2 post-hire. Risk management, not a courtesy email.

05

Replacement guarantee.

If a hire doesn't work out, we replace them. Terms agreed before the search starts.
